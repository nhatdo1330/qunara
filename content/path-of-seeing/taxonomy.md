# Qunara Path of Seeing Editorial Taxonomy
Version 1.0

Purpose

This taxonomy defines the editorial structure used to create 3,000–5,000 original reflection questions for Path of Seeing.

It is not organized around life situations.

Instead, every reflection explores a universal way of seeing experience.

---

# Design Principles

Organize around:

- how experience appears
- how the mind constructs certainty
- causes and conditions
- compassionate awareness
- returning to daily life

Never organize around:

- work
- relationships
- marriage
- children
- finance
- politics
- religion
- success
- failure

Those become examples—not categories.

# Universal Reflection Stages

S1 Notice

Become aware.

Questions emphasize observation only.

---

S2 Stay

Remain with experience.

No fixing.

No judging.

---

S3 Conditions

Investigate causes.

"What supports this?"

"What influences this?"

---

S4 Loosen

Question certainty.

Invite flexibility.

Never force conclusions.

---

S5 Perspective

View from another angle.

Different scales.

Different people.

Different time.

Nature.

Compassion.

---

S6 Connection

Expand toward compassion and interbeing.

---

S7 Release

Explore relaxing unnecessary grasping.

Never demand letting go.

---

S8 Return

Integrate into ordinary life.

Very practical.

Tiny next step.
