# QUNARA STORIES PRD

## Purpose

Stories add a human dimension to Qunara.

They are not scientific evidence.

They are not Buddhist doctrine.

They are lived experiences, historical narratives and community reflections that invite readers to pause, feel and think.

## Story types

- Personal Story
- Historical Story
- Community Story

## Editorial labels

Every story must clearly show its type.

Personal experience must never be presented as scientific evidence or canonical Buddhist teaching.

## Required story experience

Each story page should contain:

1. Hero image
2. Story category
3. Title
4. Subtitle
5. Reading time
6. Main narrative
7. What this experience taught me
8. Buddhist reflection
9. Questions for reflection
10. Related stories
11. Language switcher

## Visual style

The page should feel like a premium editorial magazine.

Use:

- real photography where available
- large serif headline
- comfortable reading width
- warm gold accents
- midnight background
- restrained animation
- short pull quotes

Avoid:

- generic AI Buddha artwork
- decorative spirituality
- mystical energy
- fake portraits
- stock photography when a real image exists

## Bilingual behavior

English route:

/en/stories/the-buddha-i-never-expected-to-receive

Vietnamese route:

/vi/cau-chuyen/buc-tuong-phat-toi-khong-bao-gio-nghi-minh-se-co

Switching language should remain on the same story.

## Content ownership

Files inside content/stories are editorially approved.

Codex may render them but may not rewrite, summarize or translate them.
