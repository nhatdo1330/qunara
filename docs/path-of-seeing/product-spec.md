# Path of Seeing
## Product Specification (v1.0)

**Product:** Qunara Practice  
**Experience:** Path of Seeing  
**Document Owner:** Product Director & Contemplative Experience Editor

# 1. Product Mission

## Purpose

Path of Seeing is a contemplative experience that invites users to slow down and observe the nature of experience through quiet exploration rather than instruction.

Instead of asking users to solve problems or analyze their lives, the experience gently encourages direct observation through a living landscape of interconnected reflection prompts.

The experience is designed to cultivate curiosity, spaciousness, and presence.

It should feel closer to wandering through a peaceful garden than completing an activity.

---

## Design Principles

The experience should always feel:

- calm
- effortless
- spacious
- beautiful
- non-judgmental
- timeless
- emotionally safe

The experience should never feel:

- educational
- analytical
- persuasive
- productivity-oriented
- goal-driven
- overwhelming

---

## Philosophical Foundation

Questions are invitations.

They are not tests.

There are no correct answers.

There is no progress score.

There is no final realization to reach.

The journey itself is the practice.

---

# 2. User Emotional Journey

## Arrival

Emotion:

> I can slow down.

The user enters a tranquil landscape.

Nothing asks for attention.

Nothing demands action.

Only one softly glowing reflection appears.

---

## Curiosity

The user gently touches the reflection.

The reflection dissolves into light.

Several nearby reflections slowly emerge.

The world feels alive.

---

## Exploration

The user follows whichever question attracts them.

Not because it is correct.

Because it resonates.

Every interaction reveals more possibilities.

The landscape feels organic rather than structured.

---

## Spaciousness

After several minutes the user naturally notices:

- there is no destination
- there is no wrong path
- every question connects to another
- thoughts come and go

The pace naturally slows.

---

## Dissolution

The questions begin to fade.

The space becomes quieter.

Particles drift away like mist.

---

## Rest

The final lotus scene appears.

Nothing remains to do.

The session ends in stillness.

---

# 3. Complete Session Timeline

## Phase 1 — Arrival (0–20 seconds)

Display:

- peaceful animated background
- subtle ambient motion
- breathing circle
- one luminous reflection

Audio:

- distant water
- light wind
- occasional bell

No instructions beyond a simple invitation.

Example:

> Touch whenever you feel ready.

---

## Phase 2 — Opening (20–60 seconds)

Touching the first node:

- node becomes light
- fades
- creates 3–5 nearby reflections
- breathing guidance begins

---

## Phase 3 — Wandering (1–4 minutes)

The user freely explores.

Every touched reflection:

- disappears
- leaves soft particles
- reveals nearby reflections

Old reflections gradually dim.

New ones emerge naturally.

The landscape continuously evolves.

---

## Phase 4 — Quieting (last minute)

The appearance of new reflections slows.

Existing reflections fade more gently.

Background music becomes more spacious.

Breathing guidance becomes less noticeable.

---

## Phase 5 — Dissolution

All remaining reflections dissolve.

Particles gather toward the center.

The world slowly brightens.

---

## Phase 6 — Lotus Reveal

A breathtaking lotus artwork slowly appears.

No achievement.

No score.

Only silence.

Optional closing reflection:

> Thank you for seeing.

---

# 4. Interaction Model

## Primary Interaction

Only one gesture exists:

Gentle tap.

No:

- dragging
- swiping puzzles
- multi-touch mechanics
- zoom requirements

---

## Node Behavior

Touch:

Question

↓

Light

↓

Particles

↓

Disappear

↓

Related reflections emerge

---

The interaction should feel like:

dropping a pebble into still water.

Never explosive.

---

## Exploration Rules

Users may:

- revisit nearby areas
- ignore reflections
- stop touching completely

The experience continues peacefully.

---

# 5. Node Visibility Rules

The experience is intentionally **not** a visible graph or mind map.

## Active Reflections

Maximum visible simultaneously:

8–14

Never hundreds.

Never dense clusters.

---

## Placement

Nodes emerge naturally around the user's current area.

Spacing adapts dynamically.

No overlapping text.

No visual collisions.

---

## Motion

Nodes gently drift.

Tiny breathing motion.

Occasional shimmer.

No constant movement.

---

## Lifetime

Untouched nodes slowly dim.

Eventually dissolve.

Recently discovered nodes remain brighter.

---

## Discovery

Only nearby reflections are revealed.

The user never sees the entire contemplative landscape.

The world should feel endless.

# 7. Breathing Behavior

Breathing guidance remains optional and non-intrusive.

No counting.

No performance.

---

Visual rhythm:

Expand

Pause

Contract

Pause

---

Suggested pace:

Approximately six breaths per minute.

The breathing indicator never demands synchronization.

It simply offers a gentle rhythm.

If the user ignores it, nothing changes.

---

# 8. Audio Behavior

Audio layers:

## Ambient

- flowing water
- distant wind
- birds (occasionally)
- insects at dusk (optional)

---

## Musical

Minimal.

Long sustained tones.

Very sparse.

No melody that attracts attention.

---

## Interaction

Touching a reflection:

soft glass tone

or

small singing bowl resonance.

---

## Bell

Occasional.

Never regular.

Never startling.

Approximately every 60–90 seconds.

---

## Silence

Silence is considered part of the soundtrack.

---

# 9. Haptic Behavior

Entirely optional.

Disabled by default if the device is in silent mode or accessibility settings request reduced feedback.

Touch:

very soft pulse.

Breathing:

optional tiny pulse at inhale.

Lotus reveal:

slightly longer warm pulse.

No vibration patterns that resemble notifications.

---

# 10. Session Ending

Default duration:

5 minutes.

Configurable:

2–15 minutes.

---

Near completion:

Questions gradually disappear.

Particles become fireflies.

Mist brightens.

Lotus slowly blooms.

---

The lotus remains visible for quiet observation.

Optional actions:

- Begin another session
- Return Home

No statistics.

No streaks.

No achievements.

No encouragement to stay longer.

---

# 11. Accessibility

Support:

- Dynamic Type
- VoiceOver
- Reduce Motion
- Reduce Transparency
- Closed captions for ambient guidance
- High contrast mode
- Color-independent interaction
- Left and right hand usage

The experience must remain complete with audio disabled.

The experience must remain complete with haptics disabled.

Animations should gracefully simplify when Reduce Motion is enabled rather than disappear abruptly.

---

# 12. Content Safety

Reflection prompts should:

- remain universally human
- avoid assumptions about the user's circumstances
- avoid directing users toward major life decisions
- avoid implying hidden knowledge or spiritual attainment
- avoid guilt, shame, or fear
- avoid evaluating the user's thoughts or actions

The experience must not:

- diagnose emotional states
- promise enlightenment
- promise healing
- encourage dependency
- replace meditation instruction
- replace professional care
- manipulate vulnerable users through urgency or exclusivity

Reflection content should be inspired by Buddhist contemplative traditions while remaining accessible to people of any or no religious background. Where ideas draw from Buddhist teachings, they should be presented as invitations to observe experience, not as doctrinal claims requiring belief.

# 13. Technical Acceptance Criteria

## Performance

- Initial experience loads in under 2 seconds on a modern mobile connection after assets are cached.
- Touch response latency is under 100 ms.
- Smooth rendering at 60 fps on supported devices, with graceful degradation on lower-end hardware.
- Battery consumption remains appropriate for sessions up to 15 minutes.
- Audio transitions occur without audible clicks or gaps.

## Interaction

- No overlapping reflection text.
- No unreachable reflections.
- Every reflection reveals at least one valid continuation until the session enters the quieting phase.
- Reflection placement remains readable across all supported screen sizes and orientations.
- Reflection appearance, motion, and disappearance feel continuous and free of visual popping.

## Content

- Reflection graph contains thousands of curated prompts across interconnected contemplative themes.
- Multiple valid paths emerge from any starting reflection.
- Consecutive prompts avoid unnecessary repetition.
- Sessions exhibit high variation, making repeated experiences feel fresh while maintaining thematic coherence.

## Reliability

- Sessions can be completed entirely offline once required assets and prompt libraries have been downloaded.
- Interrupted sessions resume gracefully or restart intentionally without corrupting state.
- Reduced Motion, audio-off, and haptics-off modes preserve the complete contemplative experience.

---

# 14. Things That Must Never Be Implemented

The integrity of *Path of Seeing* depends as much on what it excludes as on what it includes.

The experience must never include:

- quizzes or personality tests
- right or wrong answers
- text input
- chat conversations
- AI-generated replies during a session
- branching based on psychological profiling
- life-category menus (work, money, relationships, career, etc.)
- progress bars
- completion percentages
- experience points
- levels
- badges
- achievements
- streaks
- leaderboards
- daily pressure or fear of missing out
- time-limited rewards
- advertisements within a session
- pop-up interruptions
- notifications during practice
- persuasive engagement mechanics
- competitive elements
- manipulative retention techniques
- emotional sentiment scoring
- mood classification
- mental health assessments
- diagnostic language
- promises of healing, awakening, or enlightenment
- claims that the experience scientifically validates Buddhism
- claims that Buddhist teachings predict or prove modern science
- visual clutter, crowded node maps, or forced navigation

Above all, the experience must never shift the user's attention away from quiet observation toward achievement. Every design decision should preserve the feeling that the user is gently discovering a living contemplative landscape rather than interacting with software.
